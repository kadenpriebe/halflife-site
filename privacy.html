Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

half-lyfe is built so that your data stays with you. We run no server of our own, use no analytics, no advertising, and no third‑party software development kits. Everything below lives on your iPhone, in your own iCloud account, or with Apple. This policy explains what the app stores, what it reads from Apple Health, what it shares when you join a group, and what Apple handles on our behalf.

What the app stores on your device

  • Drink log. Each drink you log (name, caffeine amount, how much of it you drank, time opened and finished), your favorite drinks, your “usual” drink, and the routine the app learns from your history (for example that you tend to have a drink around 2 pm).
  • Check‑ins, reaction tests and calibration. Your “how do you feel” answers, reaction‑test results, and the caffeine half‑life and limits the app learns from them.
  • Profile. The details you enter to seed the model: weight, an optional age, sensitivity, bedtime and wake time, goal, and your answers about smoking, oral contraceptives, pregnancy, and medications that slow caffeine clearance. Every sensitive question offers “Prefer not to say”.
  • Caffeine habit. Your optional answers about how much caffeine you have on a usual day, how many days a week and for how long. They seed your tolerance estimate until your own logs take over.
  • Where you heard about us. Your answer to this onboarding question. It stays on your iPhone; we never receive it.
  • Sleep and overnight readings from Apple Health. If you connect Apple Health, the last 120 nights of sleep and (only if you turn on overnight vitals) the readings listed under Apple Health below.

This data lives in an App Group container shared only between the half-lyfe app and its own widgets, Control Center control, Live Activity and Apple Watch app on your devices. It is included in your normal iCloud or computer backup if you have backups enabled. You can export it under Profile → Support & Legal → Export data, and delete all of it at any time under Profile → Account Actions → Delete account.

iCloud sync

To keep your own iPhone, Apple Watch and Mac in step and to restore after a reinstall, the app stores your drink log and a small settings snapshot in the private database of your iCloud account using Apple’s CloudKit. Apple hosts it; we operate no server and cannot read it. The snapshot contains your drink log, favorites, usual drink, bedtime and wake time, goal, age, self‑reported sensitivity, routine, and the learned half‑life and limits. It deliberately excludes anything read from Apple Health, your reaction‑test baselines, your body weight, your answers about pregnancy, contraceptives, medications, genotype and smoking, and your caffeine‑habit answers — those stay on the iPhone that recorded them and are re‑learned or re‑entered after a restore. You can turn iCloud off for half-lyfe in iPhone Settings, and “Delete account” removes everything the app put in iCloud.

Groups

Groups are optional. If you create or join one, the app shares with the members of that group, through a shared iCloud zone: a display name you choose, an anonymous member identifier, today’s caffeine total, your current estimated level, and the name and time of your last drink. The level shown to friends is computed with the population‑average half‑life, not your personal one, so nothing learned from Apple Health reaches anyone else. Group members see this until you leave the group; owners can remove members, and “Delete account” removes your membership and every group you own.

Apple Health

Apple Health is optional. The app asks for it only from the Insights cards that use it, never during setup. If you allow it, half-lyfe:

  • Writes each drink you log to Health as dietary caffeine. Deleting a drink in the app deletes that Health entry too.
  • Reads sleep analysis to learn how long caffeine keeps you awake and to refine your bedtime cutoff.
  • Reads, only if you also turn on overnight vitals, heart rate, heart rate variability, respiratory rate, blood oxygen, wrist temperature, environmental sound levels, exercise minutes, active energy and workouts. These are used on your phone to compare caffeinated and clean nights and to leave out nights skewed by illness, noise or a late workout. Each reading’s purpose is listed in the app before you turn it on.

Health data is never stored in iCloud, never transmitted off the device, never used for advertising or marketing, and never shared with anyone. You can revoke access at any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps → half-lyfe, or turn overnight vitals off under Profile → Apple Health.

Notifications

Reminders (peak focus window, last call, bedtime load, daily limit, worn off, routine reminders, daily insight, weekly recap, morning brief and tomorrow’s plan) are scheduled locally on your device and each has its own switch. The only push notifications are silent iCloud sync signals from Apple that carry no content.

Subscriptions and payment

half-lyfe Pro is sold as an auto‑renewing subscription through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes the payment and holds your billing details; we never see your card number or Apple Account details. The app verifies your subscription status on device using Apple’s StoreKit framework. The only subscription information stored by the app is whether Pro is currently active.

Feedback and reports

“Send feedback” and “Report a member” open a mail you review and send yourself. The mail is pre‑filled with your reason, your note, the app version and iOS version, and for a report the group’s name. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect analytics, crash reports, or usage statistics.
  • We do not use advertising or tracking identifiers, and we do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to anyone.
  • We do not operate a server that receives data from the app.

Your rights

Because your data is on your device and in your own iCloud account, you can read, export and delete it yourself at any time from the app. If you have a question or a request under a privacy law that applies to you (for example the GDPR or the CCPA), email us and we will help.

Children

half-lyfe is not directed at children under 13. If you enter an age under 18 in your profile, the app applies a lower daily limit.
