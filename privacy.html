Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026. Current release: version 1.0.1, build 8 and later non-AI builds.

The current half-lyfe release plans caffeine on your device. AI planning is unavailable, is not an active subscription feature, and sends nothing to OpenAI or Cloudflare. iCloud tracking sync, friend Groups and the acquisition survey are unavailable in this release. There are no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

Data stored on your device

  • Drink names, caffeine amounts, portions, opened and finished times, favorites and your usual drink.
  • Age band, sleep schedule, caffeine habits, optional profile details and health-related answers such as sensitivity, smoking, pregnancy and medication. These seed local estimates, not diagnosis.
  • Self-reported drink effects, check-ins, optional reaction tests, model parameters, tracking streaks and break preferences.
  • Selected calendar identifiers, manual time windows, intended drinks, plans and reminder preferences. Calendar event details are read locally from calendars you authorize.
  • Up to 120 nights of imported Apple Health sleep and, when separately enabled, overnight readings.

The app and its extensions share a protected App Group container. Sensitive tracking files and preferences are marked as excluded from automatic backups; operating-system exclusions are advisory, so we cannot guarantee every backup tool honors them. There is no app-managed iCloud tracking upload in this release. Export CSV or JSON under Profile > Export data. An export can contain sensitive information; you control its destination and deletion.

Calendar

Calendar is optional. The app reads only your selected calendars through Apple's EventKit and never changes events. Google calendars appear only when synced into the iPhone's Apple Calendar database. Manual planning works without access. Calendar data is not sent to an AI provider or developer server. Disconnect or change calendars under Profile > Plan your day.

Apple Health and Apple Watch

Apple Health is optional and requested through a feature connection or settings, not required for onboarding. With permission the app writes logged drinks as dietary caffeine and reads sleep for local estimates and comparisons. Overnight vitals require a separate opt-in: heart rate, heart-rate variability, respiratory rate, blood oxygen, wrist temperature, environmental sound, exercise, active energy and workouts help compare nights and identify confounding conditions. Each purpose is listed before authorization.

Raw Health records and medical answers stay on your iPhone. A directly paired Apple Watch can receive drink logs, schedules and tracking estimates through Apple's WatchConnectivity, including model parameters derived on the iPhone. Those copies stay locally on the Watch, not in CloudKit or a developer server. Revoke Health access in the Health app; disable overnight vitals in Profile > Manage Apple Health. Comparisons and model learning do not measure caffeine clearance or prove that caffeine caused a sleep change.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally after permission. Settings are optional and editable in Profile > Reminders. Proposals never log a drink automatically.

Subscriptions

Apple processes subscriptions through StoreKit. We do not receive payment cards or Apple Account credentials. The app locally checks verified purchase transactions, renewal and trial eligibility, and caches Pro access. Purchase and Restore do not contact our AI backend. Apple handles its own processing under its privacy policy.

Support

When you voluntarily send a support email, we receive your email address, message and attachments you include; an in-app draft may include app and operating-system versions. Nothing is sent until you send it. We use this information to respond and retain it only as needed for support or legal obligations. Do not send medical records. Request deletion at halflyfesupport@gmail.com.

Retention and deletion

Tracking data stays on your device until deletion or app removal; imported sleep and vitals are limited to the latest 120 nights. Profile > Delete account clears this iPhone's local records and preferences and is also available from the paywall. For a known earlier iCloud identity, the app requests erasure and retries online after verifying the original account; keep it installed until erasure finishes. It does not guess at unknown ownership: manage unidentified previous copies in iCloud storage settings or contact support. This deletion-only CloudKit access does not upload new tracking records. Remove the app from the Watch and other devices separately. Samples saved to Apple Health remain there; manage them in Health. Exports and backups are separate copies. Deleting data does not cancel an Apple subscription; cancel in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions.

Earlier versions and development testing

Earlier builds offered private CloudKit sync and optional Groups. They could store manually logged drinks, favorites, schedules and settings in the user's iCloud, and share a chosen name, member identifier and caffeine totals with invited members. Earlier snapshots could contain model parameters. Current builds stop these uploads; stopping sync does not delete prior copies. Manage previous app data in iCloud storage settings or contact support. Development testing also used Cloudflare installation verification and optional referral/AI requests. This service is not used by the current release. Contact support to remove development records; anonymous aggregate referral totals cannot be tied back to a person.

Young users

The app is not directed at children under 13 and onboarding blocks that age band. Users aged 13-17 can track and preview intake. Local planning applies lower intake limits and excludes energy-drink recommendations; these limits are not an endorsement that caffeine is appropriate or risk-free for teens. Use with a parent or guardian's permission and seek professional guidance. Pediatric guidance recommends avoiding caffeinated drinks. No exact birth date is required. The current release does not collect teen calendar or Health information on a developer server.

Your rights and changes

Review, export and delete local information in the app. Contact us about privacy rights, prior-version data or support records. Future AI or cloud features require updated disclosures and any necessary consent before activation; a coming-soon label is not consent. Changes are posted here with an effective date.
