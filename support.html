Support

Questions, bugs, or feedback: email halflyfesupport@gmail.com.

Subscription help

Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple. To cancel or change your plan, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then half-lyfe. To request a refund, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Restoring a purchase

On the plan screen tap “Restore” while signed in to the Apple Account that made the original purchase.

Your data

The current non-AI release keeps tracking and planning locally; Groups and iCloud tracking sync are unavailable. Export drinks as CSV or data as JSON in Profile > Export data. Delete account clears this iPhone and requests deletion of any earlier CloudKit records when online. Keep the app installed until erasure finishes. Remove Watch and other-device copies separately. Delete account is also on the paywall and does not cancel your Apple subscription. Apple Health samples remain separately manageable in Health.

Earlier-version Groups

Open the group, tap the menu in the top corner and choose “Report a member”; a pre‑filled email opens, and we respond within 24 hours. To stop seeing someone, choose “Block a member” or hold their row. Group owners can also remove members under Participants.
