Subscription help

Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple. To cancel or change your plan, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then half-lyfe. To request a refund, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Restoring a purchase

On the plan screen tap “Restore purchases” while signed in to the Apple Account that made the original purchase.

Your data

Export everything as JSON under Profile → Data. Delete everything, including what the app keeps in your iCloud account, under Profile → Account → Delete account and data. Apple Health access is managed in the Health app under Sharing → Apps → half-lyfe.

Reporting a group member

Open the group, tap the menu in the top corner and choose “Report a member”; a pre‑filled email opens. Group owners can also remove members under Participants.
