Not medical advice

half-lyfe estimates how much caffeine is in your body using a pharmacokinetic model seeded from the information you provide and refined from your check‑ins, reaction tests and sleep data. These are estimates, not measurements. The daily limits, bedtime cutoffs, alertness forecasts and suggested caffeine amounts it shows are general wellness guidance drawn from published population research, adjusted to your answers; they are not tailored medical advice. The app is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Talk to a doctor about caffeine if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, under 18, have a heart condition, anxiety, a sleep disorder or high blood pressure, take medication, or have any health concern, and follow your doctor’s advice over anything the app shows.
