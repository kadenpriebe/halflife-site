Not medical advice

half-lyfe uses a pharmacokinetic model seeded from your answers. Optional sleep observations, reaction checks and self-reports can make experimental model adjustments, but do not measure caffeine clearance, establish your actual half-life or validate predictions of focus or sleep. Daily limits, bedtime projections and intended-drink planning are general wellness estimates informed by population research, not tailored medical advice or guarantees. The app is not a medical device and provides no diagnosis or treatment. Ask a clinician about caffeine if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, under 18, have a heart condition, anxiety, a sleep disorder or high blood pressure, take medication, or have any health concern. Follow professional advice over app estimates. Do not use this app to decide whether it is safe to drive, operate machinery or perform another safety-critical activity.

Earlier-version Groups

Groups let you share today’s caffeine numbers with people you invite, under a display name you choose. Choose a name that is not offensive, misleading or someone else’s, and do not use a group to harass anyone. Names are checked on your device against a word filter. Group owners can remove members, anyone can leave a group, and from the group’s menu you can block a member (they disappear from your leaderboards) or report them. Optional online planning and connected agents

Where available in your build and account, AI planning is an optional ranking of generic schedule windows, not a measurement of your body or a medical recommendation. Local checks determine whether a proposed drink and time are eligible. Local planning remains available without AI sharing. Connected agents are separately authorized to read shared future plans and, if approved, suggest coffee. Review every draft in half-lyfe before approving it. Approval is a plan, not a record that you consumed a drink; suggestions never log intake automatically.

Online features require connectivity, configured services and any applicable subscription. Paid AI ranking permits up to 20 provider attempts per day per verified subscription owner, shared across linked installations. Additional short-term and service-wide capacity limits apply, and a failed provider attempt can still count against a limit. Online availability is not guaranteed. Agent availability and its own account requirements depend on the service you choose. Open half-lyfe to receive and validate agent drafts; an overnight suggestion is not guaranteed background delivery. Disconnecting stops future sharing from this phone, but server removal may remain pending and an agent may retain its own copies under its terms and privacy policy.
