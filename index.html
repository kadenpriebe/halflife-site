half-lyfe

Energy when you need it. Plan your caffeine. Keep your bedtime in mind.

Log a drink, preview its estimated caffeine at bedtime, and arrange an intended drink around selected calendar events or manual times. Keep a history of what you drank and how you felt, with optional local Apple Health sleep comparisons.

The current release plans on your device. AI planning is coming soon, not an active paid feature. Groups and iCloud tracking sync are unavailable in this release. Estimates are modeled, not measurements, medical advice or guarantees of sleep or performance.
