half-lyfe

half-lyfe is an iPhone app that tracks caffeine the way your body actually processes it. Tap a widget, the Action Button, or Back Tap to log your usual, or search for anything else. The app learns your personal caffeine half-life from your check-ins and your sleep, then tells you when your peak focus window is, when to have your last coffee, and how much caffeine will still be circulating at bedtime.

Everything runs on your iPhone and in your own iCloud account. There is no server and no analytics. Estimates are modeled, not medical advice.
